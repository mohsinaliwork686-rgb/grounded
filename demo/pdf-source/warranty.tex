Quillbrook Cycles is a made-up bike shop. This booklet was written for the grounded demo.

\section*{Frames}
Frames are covered for 2 years from the date of purchase against defects in materials and
workmanship. Carbon frames are covered for 5 years.

\section*{Parts and components}
Parts and components, such as gears, brakes and wheels, are covered for 90 days.

\section*{E-bike batteries}
E-bike batteries are covered for 1 year or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. A
battery that holds less than 70\% of its original charge within that time is replaced.

\newpage
\section*{What isn't covered}
Normal wear isn't covered: tyres, inner tubes, chains, cassettes, brake pads and bar tape wear out
with use. Damage from crashes, misuse, racing or poor maintenance isn't covered either.

\section*{Crash replacement}
If your frame is damaged in a crash within the 2-year warranty period, you can buy a replacement
frame at 30\% off the normal price.

\section*{Making a claim}
Bring the bike and your receipt to the shop. We inspect it within 3 working days and tell you what
we can do. You need your receipt for every warranty claim.
