# Returns and refunds

Quillbrook Cycles is a made-up bike shop. This handbook was written for the grounded demo.

## Returning something you bought

You can return most items within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Items must be unused and
in their original packaging, and you need your receipt or order number.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of us receiving the item.

## Sale items

Items bought in a sale can be returned within 14 days instead of 30. They are refunded as store
credit, not money, unless they are faulty.

## Bikes

A bike can be returned within 30 days if it has been ridden less than 50 km. Bikes ridden further
than that can't be returned, but they are still covered by the warranty.

## What can't be returned

Helmets that have been worn can't be returned, for hygiene and safety reasons. Custom builds,
wheels built to order and anything we have cut to size, such as a steerer tube or brake cable,
can't be returned either.

## Faulty items

If something is faulty, you can return it at any time within the warranty period, and the 30-day
limit doesn't apply. See the warranty booklet for what is covered.
