# Repairs and servicing

## Services and prices

- A basic service costs £45. It covers gears and brakes adjusted, the chain cleaned and oiled, and
  a safety check.
- A full service costs £95. It covers everything in the basic service, plus the bike stripped
  down, cleaned, and the bearings checked and regreased.
- A puncture repair costs £12 and is done while you wait, usually within 20 minutes.

Parts are charged on top of the service price. We always ask before fitting a part that costs more
than £20.

## Booking

Book a service online or in the shop. Most services are finished within 3 working days. In March,
April and May, our busiest months, it can take up to 7 working days.

## E-bikes

We service e-bikes from the brands we sell. We don't repair e-bike motors or batteries ourselves;
we send them to the manufacturer under warranty.
