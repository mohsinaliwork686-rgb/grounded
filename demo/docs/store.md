# Visiting the shop

## Opening hours

- Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm.
- Sunday, 10am to 4pm.
- Closed on bank holidays.

## Where we are

Quillbrook Cycles, 14 Mill Lane, Ashworth. There are four free parking spaces behind the shop and a
covered bike rack at the front.

## Test rides

You can test ride any bike in stock. Bring a photo ID, and book ahead at weekends.
