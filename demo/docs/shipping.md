# Shipping

## Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom only, including the Scottish Highlands and the
islands, where delivery takes 2 extra working days.

## Delivery options and costs

- Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs £4.95.
- Standard delivery is free on orders over £50.
- Next-day delivery costs £9.95 if you order by 2pm on a working day.

## Bikes

Bikes are delivered 90% assembled. You need to fit the handlebars, pedals and front wheel, which
takes about 20 minutes with the tools included in the box. Bike delivery is always free.

## Click and collect

You can order online and collect from the shop, usually the next working day. We hold orders for
14 days.
