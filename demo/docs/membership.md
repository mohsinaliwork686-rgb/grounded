# Quillbrook Club membership

## What it costs

Membership costs £20 a year.

## What you get

- 10% off parts and accessories. The discount doesn't apply to bikes or to servicing.
- A free safety check once a year.
- Early access to our sales, one day before everyone else.

## Joining and cancelling

Join online or in the shop. If you cancel within 14 days of joining, you get a full refund. After
that, membership runs to the end of the year you paid for and isn't refunded.
